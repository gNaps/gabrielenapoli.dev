**TManager** is a platform for organizing travel for soccer teams, from
friendly matches to tournaments. It turns logistics that usually live in
spreadsheets and group chats into a guided, structured workflow.

<br />
Users are assigned to teams by role (coach, manager, staff) and plan trips step
by step: hotels, transport, schedules and budgets.

<br />
### What I built
<br />

- A front and back office in Angular, designed for fast, modular workflows.
- A backend in Fastify and Prisma, with secure access to travel and team data and support for role-based, relational data.

<br />
### My role
<br />
I handled fullstack development, from database modeling to UX, aiming for a tool
tailored to the real needs of sports organizations.
