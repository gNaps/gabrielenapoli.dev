**Regalando** (Italian for "giving a gift") makes gift-giving simple: users
create and share wishlists, while gift choices stay a surprise until the moment
they're given — no duplicates, no awkward returns.

<br />
It's available as both a mobile app and a web application, with a consistent
experience across devices.

<br />
### What I built
<br />

- A Flutter mobile app with a native-like experience on Android and iOS.
- A Next.js web app focused on speed, SEO and accessibility.
- A Supabase backend handling authentication and secure media storage.

<br />
### My role
<br />
I led development end to end, from the product experience to the cross-platform
architecture. The main challenge was keeping gift choices private without adding
friction to the UX.
