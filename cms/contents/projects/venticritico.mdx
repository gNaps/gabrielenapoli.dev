<Card>

**VentiCritico** is a campaign manager for Dungeons & Dragons. It brings the
things a table needs (campaigns, sessions and notes) into one organized,
real-time workspace.

<br />
It's built with Next.js and Convex, which keeps every player and DM in sync in
real time.

<br />
### My role
<br />
I designed and built VentiCritico end to end (the Next.js frontend, the Convex
data and real-time layer, and the UX), aiming for a tool that stays fast and
effortless during a live session.
